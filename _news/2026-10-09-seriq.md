SEMTL is becoming [SERIQ](https://seriq.ca/en/), a new software engineering research centre for Québec. It starts with four universities: Université de Montréal, Polytechnique Montréal, McGill, and ÉTS. Benoit Baudry is the director, and Houari Sahraoui championed the centre. I am proud to have restarted SEMTL in 2022 and to have helped secure support for SERIQ. I will co-lead the cyber-physical systems axis with Aren Babikian and Francis Bordeleau. Come find out more at the [Rentrée SERIQ](https://seriq.ca/en/events/2026-10-29-rentree-seriq/) on 29 October at Université de Montréal, and [register](https://event.fourwaves.com/seriq-rentree) by 23 October.
